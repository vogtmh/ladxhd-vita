Vita: colour correction and the pixel grid need the finished picture, so they used to switch the whole frame to the
PC pipeline (DrawGameRT: separate targets for the world, shadows, light and blur, plus main and final copies), which
ran at 30 fps. Now the frame is drawn the same single-pass way as without them. The pixel grid only darkens the grid
lines (colour * (1 - mask)), so it is multiplied onto the frame where it is drawn instead of copying the frame
through its shader: no extra pass. Colour correction draws the frame into the final target, which is then presented
through its shader (one extra pass).
