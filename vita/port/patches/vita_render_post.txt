Vita: colour correction and the pixel grid need the finished picture as a texture, so they used to switch the whole
frame to the PC pipeline (DrawGameRT: separate targets for the world, shadows, light and blur, plus main and
final copies), which ran at 30 fps (about 12 target switches per frame instead of 6). Now the frame is drawn the
same single-pass way as without them, only into a target instead of the screen: the world into MainRenderTarget
when the pixel grid has to scale it over, the whole frame into the final target when colour correction has to be
applied to it. That is one extra full-screen pass per effect.
