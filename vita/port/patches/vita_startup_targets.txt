Vita startup: the render targets were created (and created again for the post-load size) on the first update after
the 1.5 s black start delay, which is exactly when the intro music starts. The ~16 GXM render targets took ~0.5 s,
so the music ran dry right away and the intro stuttered. They are now created during the black delay.

                {

                        {
                            _initRenderTargets = true;
