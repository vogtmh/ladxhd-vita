Vita: the video settings get a saved Off/FPS Only/Monitor/Detailed selector in place of "Disable UI Blur".
The native mode is shared with shortcut cycling. Legacy boolean settings migrate to Off or FPS Only.
The language files have no text for it, so it falls back to built-in English strings.
